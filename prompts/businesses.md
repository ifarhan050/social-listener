```text
Set up a social-listener mission using the setup-wizard skill. Use only the social-listener tools.

=== MISSION ===
Template:            businesses
Mission name:        <<e.g. Tuition providers Lahore>>
My business:         <<e.g. home tuition for O/A levels>>
Find businesses that: <<e.g. offer tuition / academies / online tutoring>>
Offer words:         <<e.g. available, admissions open, classes, contact, WhatsApp>>
Ignore:              <<e.g. people asking for tutors, job seekers>>
Location / areas:    <<e.g. Lahore>>
Languages:           <<e.g. English, Urdu>>
Purpose:             <<competitors to watch | partners to approach | both>>

=== SOURCES ===
Platforms:           <<e.g. facebook, youtube, rss>>
Facebook groups:     <<URLs>>
Facebook pages:      <<known competitors' pages>>
YouTube channels:    <<URLs>>

=== OUTPUT ===
Report every:        <<e.g. week>>
Report folder:       <<e.g. D:\Market\competitors>>

Show me the mission (goal, search phrases, match rules, keep/exclude rules, sources, schedule) and wait for my "yes" before creating anything. Never invent URLs. Finish with what's left for me to do.
```
