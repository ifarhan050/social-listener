# Getting started: sample prompts

Copy any of these into a Claude Cowork chat. Replace the words in [brackets] with your own.

## First time
- `/setup`, or: "Set up the agent to find complaints and scams about [your brand]."
- "Set up the agent to find people asking for a [home tutor / plumber / your service] in [city or area]."
- "Log in to Facebook for the agent." (or `/connect-facebook`; you only do this once)

## Find posts
- "Find 20 negative posts about [your brand] from last month."
- "Find 10 scam posts about [your brand] from the last 7 days, Facebook only."
- "Find 15 people asking for a [service] in [city] this week."
- "Find posts about [topic] in this Facebook group: [group link] from this month."
- "Find 30 complaints about [your brand] between 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-30."
- "Search all of Facebook (no groups) for [your brand] complaints from last week."

## Results
- "Export my last search as a CSV." (or `/find export`)
- "Write a report of what the agent found for [mission] in the last 30 days."
- "Show me the 5 worst complaints this week with links."
- "Summarise what people are saying about [your brand] this month."

## Check and adjust
- "Is everything working?" (or `/status`)
- "Open the agent's dashboard."
- "Add this Facebook group: [group link]."
- "Only use Facebook and news." / "Turn off Reddit."
- "Also look for [another brand or topic]." (or `/new-mission`)
- "Update the agent."

## Good to know
- **Nothing runs in the background.** The agent only works when you ask.
- **It only reads.** It never likes, comments, joins or posts anything.
- **Your results stay on your computer:** the report (.md) and spreadsheet (.csv) are saved in your Cowork folder.
- **Facebook is read slowly, like a person.** A big search can stop partway and continue later ("continue my last search").
